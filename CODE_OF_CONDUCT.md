Contributor Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers of Last Arc: Tactics Analogue — Character Companion, we pledge to foster an open, welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project.

Our goal is to build a reliable, accessible, and community-driven digital companion that enhances the tabletop roleplaying experience.

We believe that collaboration, constructive feedback, and mutual respect are essential to creating software that serves the community.

Participation is welcomed regardless of experience level, technical background, identity, or familiarity with tabletop roleplaying games.

Our Standards

Expected Behavior

All community members, contributors, and maintainers are expected to:

Be respectful. Treat others with courtesy, patience, and professionalism.

Be constructive. Provide actionable feedback and focus criticism on ideas, implementations, and outcomes rather than individuals.

Be collaborative. Welcome contributions, suggestions, and different technical approaches.

Be inclusive. Respect different backgrounds, experiences, accessibility needs, and skill levels.

Be accountable. Acknowledge mistakes, accept constructive criticism, and work toward resolution.

Respect intellectual property. Credit original creators and respect applicable licenses, trademarks, and publishing rights.

Protect privacy. Do not disclose another person's private information without explicit permission.

Support learning. Encourage questions and help newer contributors understand the codebase.

Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or targeted hostility.

Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.

Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.

Publishing private information without consent.

Threats of violence or encouragement of harm.

Repeated disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

Deliberate sabotage, malicious code submissions, or attempts to compromise project security.

Knowingly submitting copyrighted material without appropriate authorization.

Retaliation against individuals who report misconduct in good faith.

Community Collaboration

This project welcomes contributions from developers, tabletop players, game masters, accessibility advocates, and community members.

We encourage participants to:

Search existing issues and discussions before opening new ones.

Provide clear descriptions and reproducible examples when reporting bugs.

Keep pull requests focused and explain significant implementation decisions.

Discuss major architectural changes before investing substantial development effort.

Respect maintainer decisions, even when technical opinions differ.

Assume good intentions while remaining accountable for the impact of one's behavior.

Disagreement is a natural part of software development. Respectful technical debate is encouraged; personal hostility is not.

Intellectual Property and Publisher Respect

Last Arc: Tactics Analogue — Character Companion is an independently developed community software project.

Contributors must respect the intellectual property rights of the creators and publishers of Last Arc: Tactics Analogue.

Unless appropriate permission or licensing exists, contributions must not include:

Unauthorized reproductions of copyrighted rulebook artwork.

Substantial copyrighted passages copied from official publications.

Proprietary publisher assets or materials distributed without authorization.

Content that falsely implies official publisher endorsement.

Game terminology, mechanics implementations, and compatibility features should be handled in accordance with applicable law and permissions.

The project must not misrepresent its relationship with the original game's creators or publishers.

Security and Responsible Disclosure

Security concerns should be handled responsibly.

Contributors must not publicly disclose exploitable vulnerabilities before maintainers have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and coordinate remediation.

Please report security vulnerabilities privately using the repository's published security reporting instructions.

Do not include credentials, access tokens, private keys, or sensitive personal information in issues, commits, or pull requests.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Code reviews and repository contributions.

Project-related community communication.

Public interactions where an individual is representing the project.

It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

Reports should include, when possible:

A description of the incident.

Relevant dates and communication channels.

Supporting links or screenshots.

Any additional context that may help with an investigation.

Reporting method: Use a private maintainer contact method published in the repository. Do not submit sensitive misconduct reports through public GitHub issues.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate discretion.

Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of individuals involved, although absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement decisions will consider the severity, context, and recurrence of the behavior.

Enforcement Guidelines

1. Correction

Impact: Minor inappropriate behavior or an isolated lapse in professionalism.

Response: A private or public clarification identifying the behavior and explaining the expected standard.

2. Warning

Impact: A violation involving disrespectful behavior or repeated minor incidents.

Response: A formal warning outlining the unacceptable conduct and the consequences of further violations.

3. Temporary Restriction

Impact: Serious misconduct or repeated violations after warnings.

Response: Temporary suspension from project interactions, contributions, or community participation.

4. Permanent Removal

Impact: Severe harassment, threats, malicious activity, or persistent disregard for community standards.

Response: Permanent removal from project spaces and participation privileges where maintainers have administrative authority.

Maintainers may proceed directly to stronger enforcement measures when warranted by the severity of an incident.

Appeals

Individuals subject to enforcement actions may request reconsideration through a private maintainer contact method.

Appeals should explain the basis for reconsideration and provide relevant supporting information.

Where practical, an appeal should be reviewed by a maintainer who was not directly involved in the original decision.

Attribution

This Code of Conduct is informed by the principles of the Contributor Covenant, with additional provisions tailored to open-source software development, tabletop gaming communities, intellectual property, and responsible security practices.

It is a project-specific policy rather than a verbatim adoption of a particular Contributor Covenant version.

Our guiding principle:

Build with care. Contribute with respect. Create for the community.
